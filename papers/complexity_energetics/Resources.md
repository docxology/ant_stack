# Resources and Checked Literature

## Energy-Aware Robotics

Jaramillo-Morales, Dogru, Gomez-Mendoza, and Marques (2020), *Energy estimation for differential drive mobile robots on straight and rotational trajectories* [@jaramillo2020energy], describes a platform-specific model and validation. It is methodological context, not calibration of the Ant Stack.

Sudhakar, Karaman, and Sze (2020), *Balancing Actuation and Computing Energy in Motion Planning* [@sudhakar2020balancing], introduces compute-energy-aware stopping in motion planning. The authors' primary publication page supplies the identity and scope; no unrelated conference DOI is attached.

## Computing and Physical References

Koomey, Berard, Sanchez, and Wong (2011), *Implications of Historical Trends in the Electrical Efficiency of Computing* [@koomey2011energy], concerns historical trends, not device-specific operation prices for this manifest.

Landauer (1961), *Irreversibility and Heat Generation in the Computing Process* [@landauer1961irreversibility], supplies erased-bit thermodynamic context. Shannon (1948), *A Mathematical Theory of Communication* [@shannon1948mathematical], supplies information-theoretic context. Neither reference validates a per-FLOP coefficient, whole-robot efficiency ratio, or achieved neural channel capacity.

## Active-Inference Context

Friston, Rigoli, Ognibene, Mathys, Fitzgerald, and Pezzulo (2015), *Active inference and epistemic value* [@friston2015epistemic], supplies decision-theoretic context. Its variational/expected free energy is not automatically a physical energy quantity.

## Bibliographic Provenance

Retained bibliography identities were checked against primary publisher-deposited DOI metadata or the authors' institutional publication record. The earlier duplicate/conflicting records were removed rather than assigned guessed identities. In particular, DOI `10.1038/s41467-020-16108-9` identifies Joshi and colleagues' *Accurate deep neural network inference using computational phase-change memory* (2020), not either former sparse-network/neuromorphic title. That unrelated paper is not used to support this manuscript. The former CEIMP DOI `10.1109/ICRA40945.2020.9196880` identifies a different modular-robot paper and has likewise been removed from the CEIMP citation.

Search topics such as hardware energy telemetry, locomotion profiling, sparse neural processing, sensitivity analysis, and animal tracking are research directions, not completed validation or evidence for quantitative claims. External datasets require raw-data provenance, units, species context, and a declared independent validation design before use as manuscript evidence.
